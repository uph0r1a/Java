In the quiet stretch of early morning, the city feels like it’s holding its breath. Street vendors are just 
beginning to set up, metal shutters clatter open, and the smell of fresh bread drifts through half- 
awake alleys. Somewhere, a bus sighs to a stop and then moves on again, leaving behind a brief 
pocket of silence that feels unusually large. Even the trees along the sidewalks seem to be waiting 
for the day to fully arrive, their leaves barely shifting in the still air.
