The old observatory sat quietly on the hill, its weathered dome reflecting the last traces of sunset. 
Every evening, a handful of curious visitors gathered to watch the stars emerge, each carrying a 
different reason for looking up. Some searched for familiar constellations, others hoped to catch a 
glimpse of a passing meteor, while a few simply enjoyed the stillness that came with the fading light. 
As the sky deepened into shades of indigo, the distant hum of the city seemed to disappear, leaving 
only the gentle rustle of the wind and the quiet wonder of the night.
