# Guide for the cashier

One page, enough to work at the till. Button names are written in bold type, exactly as on the screen. Anything marked "owner" is not something a cashier can do: ask the owner.

## 1. Clocking in and out

- After you sign in the till asks **Clock in?**. Press Enter to clock in, or **Not now** if you do not want to yet.
- Forgot? Open **Clock in / out** in the side bar and press **Clock in**. At the end of the shift press **Clock out** (when you **Sign out** the till also asks **Clock out?**).
- Forgot to clock out yesterday: the till stops asking once a shift has been open too long (16 hours by default). Tell the owner, who will set your real finishing time.

## 2. Opening a table and taking the order

1. Press **Orders**, pick a table on the map, press **Start order**.
2. Press a product to add it. Press the number to type a quantity. Each line has a place for a note ("less sugar, no ice..."). Products with sizes or toppings ask you to choose.
3. Ctrl+F jumps to the menu search. **Same as last time** adds the products of the customer's previous order.
4. Take-away, GrabFood, ShopeeFood: press **New order** on the matching channel. Each order has a number ("number 7") to call the customer.
5. The box **Stock is held** means other open orders have already reserved that stock. Lower the quantity or remove the line.

## 3. Customers, points and birthdays

- Type the name and phone number, or press **Pick customer** to find a regular. A customer without a phone number earns no points and cannot be found again.
- At payment, **Use points** takes points off the bill. The rates and the maximum are set by the owner (by default: 1 point per 10.000đ spent, 1 point is worth 1.000đ, at most 50% of the bill).
- When it is the customer's birthday the screen says so and a **Birthday** button appears at payment. Once per customer per day.

## 4. Taking payment

1. Press **Pay** (or Ctrl+Enter). Choose the method.
2. Cash: type **Cash received** and the till works out the change. Bank transfer or e-wallet: only tick **I have received the full amount in the account** when the money has really arrived.
3. Discount: a cashier may give up to 10% (the owner can change it) and must write a reason.
4. Voucher: type the code and press **Apply**. The till says if the code is expired, used up, or the bill is too small.
5. Gift card: type the card code and press **Check** to see the balance; press **Add another card** if the customer has several.
6. Customer pays in several ways (for example cash and card): tick **Pay in several ways** and type each amount. The main method chosen above gets what is left.
7. People at one table paying separately: **Move / Merge / Split**, choose **Split bill**, then **Pay this part**. The rest stays open on the table.
8. **Print receipt** reprints it. Receipts are always in English.

## 5. Wrong table, joining tables

**Move / Merge / Split** → **Move table** when guests change seats, **Merge tables** when two tables become one.

## 6. Cancelling an order

- Unpaid order: pick the table, press **Change status**, and confirm the box **Cancel the order being served**. Only the person who opened the order, or the owner, can cancel it.
- Paid order (the customer changed their mind, a mistake): only the owner cancels or refunds, in **Order history**. Do not give money back from the drawer without telling the owner.

## 7. Table bookings

**Reserve** shows the list. Fill in table, name, time and number of people, then **Write down**. When the guests arrive press **Seat**. If they cancel press **Cancel booking**; if they never come press **Did not come**. The till reminds you before the booking time.

## 8. Gift cards

- Selling a card: **Gift cards**, type the amount, take the cash, press **Sell the card**. Write the code that appears (like GC-XXXX-XXXX) on the card for the customer.
- Lost code or sold by mistake: tell the owner. The owner sees the list of cards and can void a card that was never used.

## 9. Locking the till, PIN

- Stepping away for a moment: press **Lock**. The cart is kept. Pick your name, type your PIN and press **Unlock**. Forgot the PIN or typed it wrong too often: sign out and sign in with your password. After 5 wrong PINs for one name that PIN is switched off, and its owner is told at the next sign-in; after 10 wrong PINs in a row on a till the PIN box closes for 15 minutes and the till opens with the password of the person who locked it.
- Set or change your PIN with the **PIN code** button in the side bar. The till also locks itself after a while without use.

## 10. Closing the shift

**Close shift**: type the cash in the drawer at the start and the cash you counted; the till shows over or short. Cash taken out of the drawer (ice, a courier) is written down with **Write down cash taken out**.

## 11. When something goes wrong

An error message, a missing order, a slow till: take a photo of the screen or write down the message and tell the owner. Do not switch the till off in the middle of a payment.
